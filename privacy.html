PRIVACY POLICY

TCMBOK Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 1 August 2026
This Privacy Policy is provided in English. An in-app summary in Chinese (中文) is available in the app's Settings screen.

What We Collect

  • ✓
    Email address and subscription status — to manage your account and authenticate you across devices
  • ✓
    Feature usage logs — to understand how the app is used and improve it over time
  • ✓
    Questions you ask the AI Guide — used only to generate your answer; never shared or sold

What We Do Not Collect

  • ✗
    Health records, medical history, or diagnoses
  • ✗
    Location data or device contacts
  • ✗
    Photos or camera access
  • ✗
    Payment card details — all payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) and are never seen by us

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • ✓
    Authenticate you and keep your account active across devices
  • ✓
    Power the AI Guide feature and generate contextual answers to your TCM questions
  • ✓
    Send optional daily wellness tips via push notification (you can opt out at any time in Settings)
  • ✓
    Prioritise new features based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns

Third-Party Services

We share minimal data with these trusted providers, strictly for the purpose stated:

  • ✓
    Clerk — identity and authentication; stores your email address and manages secure sign-in
  • ✓
    RevenueCat — subscription management; receives your App Store / Play Store purchase receipt to verify your subscription status
  • ✓
    Apple App Store / Google Play Store — process all payments; we never receive your payment details
  • ✓
    DeepSeek AI — processes the questions you send to the AI Guide to generate answers; queries are not used for training

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal data to any other third party.

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis. We regularly review our security practices to protect your information.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or permanently delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

TCMBOK is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
